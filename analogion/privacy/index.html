Analogion privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026.

Analogion is an Orthodox Christian prayer app for iPhone, published by its developer (“we”). This policy says what the app does with information, in plain words. The short version: there are no accounts, almost everything stays on your phone, and we do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do in the app is stored on your device: your Church and calendar settings, the prayers and program days you mark as done and your history of prayer, your favourite prayers, your Jesus Prayer counts, your journal lines, the names on your prayer list, your own rule of prayer, your place in the Psalter and in each text, your name day, your confession notes and the date of your last confession, your reminder times, your reader’s text size and translation, and any recordings you download. This data never leaves your phone unless you back up your phone with Apple’s iCloud Backup or to a computer, which is under Apple’s privacy policy and your control. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

You can save a backup file from the app (Me, Backup) and restore it on another phone. The file is made on your device and goes only where you choose to send or save it; we never receive it. It holds your settings, your days and history of prayer, your progress in the programs and the Psalter, your journal, your favourites, your prayer list, your rule, your name day and your Jesus Prayer counts, and a random mark that lets the app tell a backup of the same phone from one made on another, so counts are not added twice. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

Your confession notes are never put in the backup file, and they are left out of iCloud and computer backups of your phone as well, so a new phone starts without them. They are kept in a separate file that iOS encrypts while your phone is locked, they are hidden whenever the app is not in front, they are erased when you record a confession, and you can put them behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. If you turn that lock on, the check is done by iOS on your device; the app never sees your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Widgets read a small file the app writes on your device, with the coming month of fasts and feasts and your program day. Siri and Shortcuts requests are handled on the device by Apple’s frameworks; we do not receive them.

Subscriptions

Analogion Premium is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment and keeps your billing details; we never see your card, your name or your Apple ID email.

To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created on your device (not your name, email or Apple ID), your App Store purchase receipt, the app version, the iOS version, the device model and your App Store country, and, as with any connection over the internet, your device’s IP address. RevenueCat uses this to validate purchases, restore them on a new phone and tell the app what you are entitled to. RevenueCat is based in the United States and processes this information there; its privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. We do not use this information for advertising and do not link it to anything else.

Anonymous usage counts

To see which parts of the app are used, the app sends anonymous usage counts to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service built not to identify people. A count names something done in the app and, at most, which tab, prayer, reading, program or kathisma it concerned: for example “a prayer was opened” or “day 7 of a program was finished”. It may also say which Church calendar you chose and where the plans were opened from. With each count go technical details: the app and iOS versions, the device model and screen size, your language, region and time zone, display and accessibility settings such as dark mode and text size, and how often and how long the app has been used. They come with a random identifier made for the app on your phone and scrambled there before it is sent, so that people can be counted without being known.

A count never contains anything you write, the names on your prayer list, your name day, your confession notes or what you search for. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses. Its privacy policy is at https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy.

You can turn the counts off at any time in Me, under Privacy, “Share anonymous usage counts”; from then on the app sends none.

Apart from these counts, the app sends no usage data, analytics or crash reports to us or to anyone else. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage figures, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Recordings

Recorded prayers, where the app offers them, are downloaded from our file server, audio.analogion.app, when you first play or download them, and kept on your phone. The server is run for us by Cloudflare. Like any web request, a download shows the server your device’s IP address and which file was asked for. The server does not know who you are, and we do not use these addresses to identify anyone. Cloudflare’s privacy policy is at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/. A version of the app without recordings downloads nothing.

Notifications

Reminders, the evening fast note and feast-day notes are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to a push service, and you can turn each one off in the Me tab or in iOS Settings.

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use them only to answer you, and we delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

What we do not do

We do not require or offer accounts. We do not collect your name, contacts, photos, location or health data. We do not show advertising. We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone, and we do not use any data for tracking as Apple defines it, so the app never asks for tracking permission.

Children

Analogion is rated 4+ and may be used by children with their family’s guidance, including through Family Sharing. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, beyond what this policy describes.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what information about you we hold, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the address below. Almost everything is on your phone and in your hands: deleting the app removes it. To have RevenueCat delete the purchase records tied to your random identifier, write to us and we will make the request. Apple keeps its own records of your purchases under its privacy policy.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, data protection law gives you the rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your data protection authority. We rely on our contract with you to check and restore your subscription through RevenueCat, and on our legitimate interest in delivering recordings, in counting anonymously what is used so that we can improve the app, and in answering your messages. You can object to the counts by turning them off in Me.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://haris-hr.github.io/apps/analogion/privacy/ with a new effective date, and material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
