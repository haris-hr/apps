Fortyfall

A daily planet merge puzzle for iPhone.

Support

A question, a problem or an idea? Write to support.desk.global.outreach@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and which iOS version you use.

How do I play?

Tap to drop a planet. Two planets of the same kind merge into the next one up. If a planet comes to rest above the line, the game is over. Everyone gets the same 40 planets each day, and one try.

When does the next game arrive?

At midnight, by your iPhone's clock. The result screen counts down to it.

Where are my scores kept?

On your iPhone: they are not synced to your other devices. Deleting the app deletes your scores, streak and milestones. If you are signed in to Game Center, your daily and endless scores also go to its leaderboards.

How do I see the leaderboards?

Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings. The result screen then shows your place today, and "Boards" opens the leaderboards.

Why does the app ask to add to Photos?

Only when you choose to save a result picture or a video clip to Photos. The app can add to your library; it cannot see what is in it.

Privacy policy

Fortyfall collects no personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection. The only thing it sends is anonymous usage counts, described below, which you can turn off.

What stays on your iPhone

Your games, scores, streak, milestones and replays are saved on your iPhone. The app never sends them anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone, your own backup may include them, as with any app's data.

Anonymous usage counts

To see how the game is played, the app counts games started, finished and shared and the screens opened (how to play, scores, past days, leaderboards, privacy), with at most the mode, the day, the score, the biggest planet and whether the sound is on. The counts go to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service built not to identify people. With each count go technical details: the app and iOS versions, the iPhone model, your language and region, and an identifier for your installation that is scrambled (hashed) on your iPhone before it is sent, so that players can be counted without being known. A count never includes a name, your Game Center name, a friend code or anything you type. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses; its privacy policy is at telemetrydeck.com/privacy. We use the counts only as totals, to improve the game, and never sell or share them.

You can turn the counts off at any time: on the start screen tap Privacy, then "Share anonymous usage counts", Off. From then on the app sends none.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple's game service, the app sends the score of each daily and endless game to Game Center's leaderboards, where other players see it with your Game Center name. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy for Game Center. We receive nothing from it beyond what any player sees on the leaderboards. To stop, sign out of Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

Sharing

When you copy a result, or save or share a result picture or a video clip, the text or file goes only where you send it. Saving to Photos happens only when you choose it, after iOS has asked you.

Children

Nothing that identifies anyone is collected, whatever their age; the anonymous usage counts are the same for every player.

Changes

If a later version of the app changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released.
