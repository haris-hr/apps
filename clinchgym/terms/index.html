What ClinchGym is

ClinchGym is a place to practise sales calls. An AI plays the buyer, and the app scores how you handled the call.

  • It is practice, not advice. Scorecards and suggested answers are feedback on a practice call. They are not legal, financial or professional advice, and following them does not guarantee a sale.
  • The AI can get things wrong. The prospect can say things a real buyer would not, and a scorecard can miss a good line or pass a weak one. You can report a prospect's line and dispute a check from the scorecard.
