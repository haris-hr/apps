ClinchGym help and contact

Write to support.desk.global.outreach@gmail.com. It helps to say which iPhone you have, its iOS version, and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Your subscription

  • To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or tap Manage subscription on the ClinchGym Pro page in the app. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • A free trial, when one is offered, ends without a charge if you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
  • Refunds are made by Apple, not by us: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • On a new phone, or after reinstalling the app, open ClinchGym Pro from Home or Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Your practice and your data

  • Your calls, scores and prospects are kept on your phone. They do not move to a new phone.
  • To delete everything on your phone, open Settings in the app and tap Delete all data, or Delete data on this device when you are signed in.
  • Anonymous usage counts can be turned off in the app's Settings, under Privacy.

Something in a call looks wrong

  • If the prospect says something wrong or out of character, tap Report under that line in the scorecard transcript.
  • If a scorecard check looks wrong, open its skill on the scorecard and tap Not right. Your score then counts it your way.
  • A call stopped for abusive or self-harm language is held for review instead of scored.
