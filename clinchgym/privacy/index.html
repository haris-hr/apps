ClinchGym privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

ClinchGym is published by its developer (“we”). It uses your practice calls to coach you, keeps typed practice on your phone, sends live calls only to the services that run and score them, and never sells your data or shows ads.

What we collect

We collect what the app needs to run practice calls, and a little about how the app is used. Nothing is collected for advertising.

  • Account. Your email address, so we can send you a one-time sign-in code. Your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure keychain.
  • Setup answers. Your first name (optional), what you sell, who you sell to, the selling framework you use and your birth year. These stay on your phone. What you sell and who you sell to also make your first practice prospect. We keep only the year, to check you are 16 or over, and note when you gave your consent in setup.
  • Prospects you build. For each one: who the buyer is, an optional first name, what you are selling them, the kind of call, their objections and anything else you add about them. These stay on your phone. On a live call with one, its details go with the call, so the AI can play and score that prospect, and they are kept with the call's record on our server. We suggest describing a kind of buyer, not a real, named person.
  • Real call results. After a warm-up, the app asks how the real call went. Your answer (won, lost, still open or did not happen) is kept with that prospect's title and the date of the warm-up. If you say when the call is, that time is kept too. You can also log a call yourself, with a few words of your own about who it was with and the day it was on. If you say what lost a call, by picking one of the prospect's objections or in a few words of your own, that is kept with it. These stay on your phone.
  • Practice calls. What you and the AI prospect said, when each line was said, your scores, any checks on a scorecard you dispute and your progress (streaks, levels, drills). These stay on your phone, which keeps your last 200 calls. Live call transcripts are also sent out to be scored.
  • Live voice calls. Your microphone audio is streamed to OpenAI while the call runs, so the AI prospect can hear and answer you. We do not record or store audio. OpenAI turns your speech into the transcript you see.
  • Call records on our server. For live calls only: which scenario, level and call type you chose, when the call started, its status and its outcome, and for a prospect you built, that prospect's details. We use these to apply your daily allowance and to score each call once.
  • Reports. When you report a prospect line, we keep that line, the call it came from and when you reported it. For now these stay on your phone.
  • Usage events. Which screens you open, and actions such as finishing setup, signing in, starting a call, getting a scorecard, building a prospect, starting a warm-up, opening the Pro page, subscribing and restoring a purchase. Each event carries a random identifier made on your phone, the app's version and your phone's system and version. Some carry a little about the action: the type of call, its level, whether it was live or typed, how it ended, or which plan. They never carry what you or the prospect said, your notes, a prospect's details, your name or your email address. They go to TelemetryDeck. You can turn them off in Settings, under Privacy.
  • Subscriptions. If you subscribe to Pro, Apple takes the payment. The app asks Apple which plan is active and when it renews, and keeps that answer on your phone. We never see your card details or your Apple ID.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider keeps standard request logs, such as IP addresses, for up to 30 days.

We do not use your camera, contacts, location or photos.

How we use it

We use your data only to run and improve the app for you.

  • Running calls. To play the AI prospect, which reacts to what you say.
  • Scoring. Typed calls are scored on your phone. Live calls are scored by Anthropic's Claude, which reads the transcript and quotes the lines that earned each check.
  • Progress. To show your scores over time, your streak and the levels you have opened.
  • Allowances and Pro. To count the reps and calls you use each day and month, and to give Pro's allowances while you subscribe.
  • Improving the app. Usage events show which parts of the app are used and where people stop, so we know what to fix.
  • Safety. To stop calls with abusive or self-harm language and hold them for review (see Safety reviews).
  • Support. To answer you when you write to us.

We do not use your calls to train AI models. We do not build advertising profiles or sell or share your data with advertisers.

Safety reviews

A call with abusive language, or language suggesting you might hurt yourself, is stopped, not scored and held for review. For a live call, a person on our team may then read its transcript.

  • How a call is flagged. The app checks your lines for set phrases. On live calls, the AI prospect and the AI that scores the call can also flag it.
  • Live calls. The transcript goes to a review queue that only our team can open. We use it to check the decision and to improve the app's safety.
  • Typed calls. A held typed call stays on your phone; nobody else reads it.
  • Self-harm. If you mention hurting yourself, the app shows crisis support: 988 in the US and Canada, and findahelpline.com elsewhere.

We do not contact you or anyone else about a held call, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

You can delete everything from the app's Settings, and deleting your account removes what our database holds about you at once.

  • On your phone. Your setup answers, prospects, calls, scores, real call results and reports stay until you tap Delete data on this device in Settings or remove the app. The app keeps your last 200 calls.
  • Your account. Tap Delete account in Settings. Your account, call records, allowance and any held-call transcripts are deleted from our database at once. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are removed within 30 days.
  • Held calls. A held-call transcript is kept until it has been reviewed, then deleted within 30 days.
  • Usage events. They are not linked to your account, so we cannot pick out yours to delete. TelemetryDeck says it expects to delete events after 7 to 10 years.
  • Services we use. OpenAI keeps what it processes for up to 30 days to check for abuse. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days, unless its safety systems flag it, in which case it may keep it for up to 2 years.

Your choices and rights

You decide how much leaves your phone, and you can ask us for a copy of your data or to delete it at any time.

  • Practise privately. Typed practice with the scripted prospect never leaves your phone.
  • Usage events. On unless you turn them off: Settings, Privacy, Share anonymous usage. While it is off, the app sends none.
  • Microphone. Live calls need it. You can turn it off for ClinchGym in your phone's Settings at any time.
  • Reminders. The app can remind you of two things: to say how a real call went, about two hours after a warm-up, and to practise on a day your streak is about to end. Each is off until you turn it on. Both are made on your phone. The first names the prospect you warmed up for, and the second says how long your streak is. The first has buttons to answer with, which also work from the lock screen. Your choices and the time you pick stay on your phone. We send no push notifications, and nothing leaves your phone for them. Turn each one off in the app's Settings, or turn off notifications for ClinchGym in your phone's Settings.
  • Subscriptions. Change or cancel Pro in your App Store account settings, or from Manage subscription in the app.
  • Delete. Use Delete data on this device or Delete account in the app's Settings, as described above.
  • Access, correct or export. Email us at support.desk.global.outreach@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.
  • Where you live. Depending on your country or state (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or in California), you may have more rights: to object to or restrict how we use your data, to move it, and to complain to your data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context advertising.

Children

ClinchGym is for people 16 and over. Setup asks for your birth year and does not let anyone younger continue; only the year is kept, on your phone. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you think a child has used the app, email us at support.desk.global.outreach@gmail.com and we will delete their data.

Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and strict limits on who can read it, though no system is perfectly secure.

  • Every connection between the app, our server and the services above is encrypted.
  • Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure keychain.
  • In our database, each account can read only its own records, and only our team can open the review queue.
  • A live call gets a short-lived key that works for that one call, so the app never holds our long-term keys.

Changes and contact
